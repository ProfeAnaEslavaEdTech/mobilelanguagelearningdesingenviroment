Privacy Policy — Language Passport

Last updated: 26 July 2026

In short: Language Passport is privacy-friendly. It runs on your device, works offline, needs no account, and does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

What the app stores

  • On your device only: your progress (coins, passport stamps, souvenirs, rank) and your light/dark preference are saved in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Clearing app data or tapping “Restart my journey” erases it.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no login.

Microphone (optional)

If you use Speak practice, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition to transcribe your voice on your device / via your browser's speech service. Audio is not recorded or stored by Language Passport.

Third-party service — “Grace” (optional)

The optional Grace feature opens ChatGPT, a separate service by OpenAI. If you choose to use it (after an in-app notice), you leave Language Passport and any messages you send are handled by OpenAI under OpenAI's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. It may require an OpenAI account and possibly a paid plan. Do not share personal or sensitive information there. We do not receive or store those conversations.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change accordingly.
