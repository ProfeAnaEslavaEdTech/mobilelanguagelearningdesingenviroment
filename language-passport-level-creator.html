How to use: work top-to-bottom. Set the lesson length in §1 — the guide shows how much content to add. Use + Add to grow the lists (vocabulary, chunks, dialogue, drills, culture cards…). Hit Save (JSON) to keep your work or hand it off for the build; Load to resume. Your progress also auto-saves in this browser. Fields marked * are the minimum to build a lesson.
Theoretical framework & attributions — the scholars behind this method

This Curriculum stands on established scholarly frameworks and design standards. Each method below is credited to its originator, out of respect for their teaching and design approaches. The Curriculum's own contribution is the synthesis of these approaches into one mobile, gamified language-learning method — that synthesis is the copyrighted work (see LICENSE); the underlying frameworks remain the intellectual contributions of their respective authors.

1 Lesson basics

G-CLIL Ring 1 — Casañ-Pitarch, 2017 · disciplinary genre / real-world scenario

2 Learning objective

CTL — Johnson, 2002 · action-verb objective + benefit + authentic “excellent achievement”

3 CLIL 4 Cs

CLIL 4Cs — Coyle, 2007 · every lesson should touch all four

4 Narrative & identity

G-CLIL Ring 3 — Casañ-Pitarch, 2017 · narrative transportation + identity arc + AI mentor (AWJ, 2025)

5 Vocabulary — animated images

IPO Input — Casañ-Pitarch, 2017 · dual-coding (Mayer): each word has an image + a named entrance animation the “hint” replays (§5). Add as many as the lesson length allows.

6 Lexical chunks & expressions

Lexical Approach — Lewis, 1993 · ready-made “chunks of words” with their pragmatic function (requesting, thanking, softening…). These carry fluency and politeness.

7 Model dialogue

IPO Input / ESA Study — Casañ-Pitarch; Harmer, 2007 · the example conversation (read aloud with TTS)

8 Processing drills

IPO Processing / ESA Study — Casañ-Pitarch; Harmer · learners manipulate the language (earn coins )

Gap-fill

Write the sentence, mark the blank with ___

Drag-match pairs

9 NPC mission — the assessment

IPO Output / ESA Activate — Casañ-Pitarch; Harmer · CTL authentic assessment (Johnson) — perform before the energy meter empties

Mentor hints

10 Reflection — choose your skill

IPO Output / REACT Transferring — Casañ-Pitarch; CORD–Crawford, 2001 · learner picks ORAL or WRITTEN to deploy the language in a new context

Oral prompts (recorded via speech recognition)

11 Culture & pragmatics cards — ¿Sabías que…?

CLIL Culture (Coyle) + pragmatic competence (Kasper & Rose, 2001) · the lesson ends here (as the original De Tapas did) — teach art , food , culture , curiosidad and pragmática about the place (read aloud on tap).

12 Rewards & economy

G-CLIL Ring 4 — Casañ-Pitarch, 2017 · every mechanic ties back to a learning aim
